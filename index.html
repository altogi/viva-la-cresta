How to ride

Tap ahead of the rider to slide back onto the knives, or forward again.

Hold left or right to steer: arms on the knives, a turn of the head when forward.

Hold the bottom to rake: left foot, both, or right foot. Rakes bite hardest on the knives.

Running start: tap RUN quickly after the bell.

Keyboard: ↑ knives · ← → steer · Z X C rake · Space run
